Research & work / Democratizing finance

Democratizing finance.

Intelligent tools that support informed financial decisions for people and institutions.

Our approach

Financial empowerment.

The financial landscape in Africa is undergoing a transformative shift. We work at the front of this shift, aiming to empower individuals and financial institutions with intelligent tools to make informed financial decisions.

  • Financial data collection systemA system that leverages mobile phones to collect financial data from individuals and micro-entrepreneurs who may lack traditional banking records, helping them build creditworthiness and access financial services.
  • Market information analysisReal-time market intelligence for decision-makers to analyze trends, identify emerging opportunities, and make informed financial choices based on financial profile and market conditions.
  • Social data scoring for financial inclusionTraditional credit scoring models have limits in Africa. Our approach uses social data analysis to assess creditworthiness and risk more inclusively, helping smallholder farmers and SMEs with limited credit history access loans and insurance.
