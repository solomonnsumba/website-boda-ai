Healthcare delivery.

AI-powered approaches to predict disease outbreaks, optimize interventions, and streamline care.

Our approach

Transforming healthcare delivery with AI.

The COVID-19 pandemic exposed the critical need for robust disease surveillance systems in Africa. A lack of real-time data on disease burden hinders containment efforts and worsens the impact of disease. We work on AI-powered approaches designed to predict disease outbreaks, optimize interventions, and streamline healthcare delivery.

  • Real-time disease surveillanceReal-time data collection systems gather information on disease prevalence and spread. AI models analyze this data to predict outbreaks and inform targeted interventions, enabling healthcare officials to act swiftly and effectively.
  • AI-powered drug monitoring with computer visionAI-driven image recognition helps improve drug distribution and prevent stockouts. Village health workers can use a mobile app to identify, assess, and validate medications using their smartphones, helping ensure patients receive the correct medication.
  • Mobile app for Village Health TeamsA mobile app designed for Village Health Teams (VHTs) to collect and report health-related data efficiently, improving data quality and accessibility for public health officials.
