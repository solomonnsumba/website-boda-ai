Speech & text.

Speech and text recognition and translation for Africa's diverse linguistic landscape.

Our approach

Bridging the African language diversity gap.

In Africa's diverse linguistic landscape, language barriers can limit access to information, resources, and participation in online communities. Boda AI Factory works to dismantle these barriers with speech and text recognition, translation models, and AI-powered tools.

  • Speech and text data collection & translationWe build data collection and translation systems that capture and translate speech and text across various African languages.
  • Real-time language translation with local APIsLocalized language translation APIs enable seamless, real-time communication across language barriers.
  • AI-powered chatbots & report generationCustom chatbots and report generation tools that use AI to understand user sentiment and generate reports in the appropriate language.
