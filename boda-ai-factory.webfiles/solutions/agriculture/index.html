Agriculture & livestock.

AI-powered tools for the agricultural value chain, including mobile technology for farmers and extension workers.

Our approach

Revolutionizing agriculture and livestock management with AI.

For centuries, agriculture and livestock farming have been the lifeblood of African economies. Today, with the rise of digital technologies and AI, Boda AI Factory empowers stakeholders across the agricultural value chain to automate services and unlock new levels of efficiency, leveraging mobile technology to equip extension workers and farmers with practical AI-powered tools.

  • Scalable real-time data collection and mappingOur AI-powered systems gather and analyze real-time data on agricultural landscapes, enabling comprehensive crop health monitoring, early detection of disease outbreaks in livestock, and informed decision-making for farmers and extension workers.
  • Automated yield predictionOur AI models analyze historical data, weather patterns, and current field conditions to predict crop yields, empowering farmers to make data-driven decisions about resource allocation, harvesting schedules, and market strategies.
