Business operations.

Decision-making tools that help African businesses streamline operations and allocate resources.

Our approach

Tailored business solutions for Africa.

Across Africa, businesses navigate a dynamic and competitive landscape, where strategic decisions often involve complex trade-offs between multiple objectives and resource constraints. We build AI solutions designed for the African context, helping businesses streamline operations, optimize resource allocation, and unlock efficiency through intelligent decision-making tools.

  • Route and path optimizationTraditional routing algorithms often struggle with Africa's complex infrastructure, including unpaved roads and dynamic traffic patterns. Our approach accounts for these factors, optimizing delivery routes for efficiency and cost savings.
  • Workforce schedulingScheduling tools that analyze employee skill sets, project requirements, and real-time demand fluctuations to help businesses plan for smoother operations.
  • Vendor selectionTools that analyze data across cost, quality, and reliability criteria to provide insights and recommendations for informed vendor selection.
  • Product development prioritizationAnalysis of market demand, competitor insights, and resource constraints to help businesses focus resources on the products with the highest potential for success.
