About Boda AI Factory

Who we are.

Rooted in Africa, we apply artificial intelligence and data science to the everyday challenges facing agriculture, healthcare, infrastructure, finance, and business across the continent — built with an understanding of local context, not adapted from it.

We apply artificial intelligence to everyday problems in agriculture, healthcare, infrastructure, finance, and business — sectors that shape daily life across Africa. Rather than adapting solutions built elsewhere, we start from local data, infrastructure, and constraints, and work with tech firms, NGOs, and government bodies to turn that research into tools people can actually use.

Tech firms NGOs Government bodies Researchers Development partners

Mission

To deploy AI solutions built for African sectors and contexts, helping organizations work more efficiently and deliver better services.

Vision

An Africa where communities, businesses, and governments have the AI tools they need to make better decisions, on their own terms.

Why work with us

Localized, tailored, and accountable.

  • Localized expertise

    Our team leverages years of applied research to build solutions with African data, ensuring they are relevant and effective in the contexts they serve.

  • Tailored AI solutions

    We don't offer one-size-fits-all solutions. We partner with you from concept to implementation, ensuring the AI solution integrates with your existing infrastructure and delivers measurable results.

  • Culturally sensitive AI

    We go beyond the data — our team understands the cultural nuances that influence technology adoption across African communities.

  • Full-spectrum support

    We go beyond development, offering strategic guidance, infrastructure management, and ongoing maintenance and monitoring to help AI solutions thrive over time.
