About Boda AI Factory

Who we are.

Rooted in Africa, we apply artificial intelligence and data science to the everyday challenges facing agriculture, healthcare, infrastructure, finance, and business across the continent — built with an understanding of local context, not adapted from it.

Our organization leverages the potential of artificial intelligence to address everyday challenges across diverse sectors such as agriculture, healthcare, infrastructure, and business. Through strategic exploration of AI opportunities, we aim not only to solve these challenges but also to enhance efficiency. Our approach to implementing AI solutions is tailored to the African context, reflecting our understanding of the continent's unique needs and infrastructure. We empower African entities — from tech firms and NGOs to governmental bodies — to unlock the potential of AI and data science through localized research, data, and models.

Tech firms NGOs Government bodies Researchers Development partners

Mission

We aim to advance Africa's development by deploying tailored artificial intelligence solutions across sectors, empowering organizations to enhance efficiency, maximize impact, and deliver exceptional services — driving sustainable growth and development.

Vision

To catalyze Africa's transformation through the strategic integration of artificial intelligence, empowering communities, businesses, and governments to realize their full potential and foster sustainable development.
